-  practice being the last to speak
   + coming into a meeting, stating a problem, not giving an opinion, not playing poker, not letting anybody know what you think as they speak, asking questions only out of curiosity, literally be the last to speak, Not only do you make people feel like their opinions matter, but you get the benefit of new ideas, you can still make the same decision you wanted to make at the beginning, but you get all that additional benefit.
