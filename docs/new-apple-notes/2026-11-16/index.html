整合 individuation - 再发挥自身优势的同时，接纳并训练自己身上不常用的另一面

投射 - 把内心未开发的特质，强行安在别人身上，期待对方替自己完成

真正的健康关系， 是两个完整的人在并肩同行

人共有两种核心能力 - 行动力 Agency 与 连结力 Communion

- 行动力  - “我是谁，我要去哪里？”  个体的独立与掌控
    +    体现为 ”清晰的自我意识“ 与 ”健康的边界感“

-  连结力 - “我们是谁，我们在一起“  群体的归属与融合
    +    ”看见他人， 接纳他人并与之产生情感共振“的能力。
      它让关系有温度，而不是冷冰的利益交换或规则执行

-  温和而坚定
    +    清晰地表达自己的立场和需求（行动力）， 
          同时在表达的过程中， 始终照顾到对方的感受和尊严 (连结力）

    +    把“情绪反应” 翻译成 “真实需求”
           -  当发现自己感到烦躁，发火或逃避时， 
               先停下来问自己 ”我现在除了生气，底层的情绪是什么？ 是委屈，害怕被忽视，还是觉得太累了？“

           -   试着用温和的语言把这份脆弱说出来
               ”我今天工作太累了， 现在有点消化不了这么多信息，我们晚点再聊好吗？“

    +    带着 ”柔软“ 去坚持原则
           -  成熟的边界感是温和而坚定的 
                既能真诚地共情对方的难处（”我理解你的着急“）， 
                又能清晰，客观地表达自己的决定 （”但我这周末确实有自己的安排，没法过去“）

According to Jung, a mature man is someone who has developed his masculinity to maturity And also brought up the femininity hidden in his unconscious and made it his own. So he knows how to use both the strength to achieve goals and uphold principles and the ability to feel emotions and empathise.

Someone who stands his ground but also respects the other person. - someone who listens all the way through to understand why the others think that way and can at least say, “I can see it that way too.” - having a developed anima means there’s also a relationship-oriented side to him, so he’s good at upholding both his own principles and the relationship

 -  someone whose masculinity is lacking
      +   He was just saying he thought one thing, but the moment a woman pushes back, he switches to the opposite option.
            He’s a bit lacking in a sense of self.

 -   some whose masculinity is strong but the anima is underdeveloped
      +   someone who immediately starts arguing in that situation and tries to win.

A manly man is a man who is goal-oriented, establishes order and rules, and has reason and logic. A truly mature man is one who hones his masculinity while also bringing out his hidden anima and developing it into maturity. He is someone who can recognise and manage his emotions, empathise, and build relationships.

A feminine woman is relationship-oriented and good at using emotions and empathy. A truly mature woman is one who has also developed her hidden animus into maturity. She is someone who knows what she wants and can take the initiative to achieve it.

When the anima or animus is immature, it is first projected onto the opposite sex. You project your own opposite traits onto someone else, which creates a strong attraction. Men like women who respond warmly because men struggle to express their own emotions, And women express those emotions for them.

When a woman’s animus is immature, she projects her own masculinity onto men. She is drawn to men who show the initiative or decisiveness she struggles to use herself.

A man with a mature anima is someone who can make femininity his own without discarding his masculinity. He can recognise his own emotions and express them in words, and he is attracted to women without being controlled by them.
