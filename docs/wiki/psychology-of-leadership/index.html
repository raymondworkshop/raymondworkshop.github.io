psychology-of-leadership

[AI Synthesis]: Leadership here is a mental game played with other people. You inspire them after you understand yourself. This highlight set covers principles 1–9 on goals, principle 13 on what matters in execution, and principles 14–15 on personality.

Inspire after you understand yourself

  • A leader’s job is to inspire others.
  • Put on your oxygen mask before helping others. Understand yourself so you can understand others.
  • The work is empathy, leading by example, and personal resilience.

What has to come together

  • Mastery over ego, relationships and community, sport as a meritocracy, and engagement and flow.
  • The standard under the scoreboard is whether you played to the best of your abilities.

Actions

  • Before you try to inspire or read someone else, check your own state first.
