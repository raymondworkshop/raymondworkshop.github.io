appealing-fiction

[AI Synthesis]: Under limited control and high stakes, we believe stories we want to be true. Incomplete views become complete narratives—and that gap is dangerous.

Wanting truth to be true

  • Many things feel true because we desperately want them to be true.
  • An appealing fiction appears when you are smart, want solutions, but face limited control and high stakes.
  • People chase huge outcomes partly because the possibility exists.
  • The biggest risk: wanting something to be true so badly that forecasts miss reality entirely.

Protect with error, flexibility, independence

  • Room for error, flexibility, and financial independence are indispensable defenses.
  • The bigger the gap between what you want to be true and what you need to be true for an acceptable outcome, the more you protect yourself from appealing financial fiction.

Incomplete views, complete stories

  • Everyone has an incomplete view of the world but forms a complete narrative to fill the gaps.
  • People tell coherent stories from little they know.
  • Most do not realize they misunderstand because explanations fit their limited experience.
  • We want the complicated world to make sense—so we tell stories to fill blind spots.

Stories vs statistics

  • The confident person who “knows” from what he sees can be wholly wrong because he cannot know stories inside everyone else’s head.

Actions

  • When stakes are high, widen room for error before trusting a story that feels satisfying.
  • List what you do not know explicitly before acting on a narrative that “makes sense.”
