mirroring-and-matching

[AI Synthesis]: Charm grows from flexibility: synchronize speech and body language, see the topic from the other person’s point of view, and practice until responses are automatic under real conditions.

Harmony and other people’s eyes

  • People with charm avoid being inflexible and judgmental; they enjoy seeing the world through others’ eyes as well as their own.
  • A secret of charm: being able to see the world through the other person’s eyes.
  • Work in harmony by synchronizing movements or behaviors.

Practice and doing it

  • Practice every skill on every occasion; focus on one skill at a time — with family, friends, acquaintances, and coworkers.
  • Thinking, getting ready, practicing, and visualizing are not doing it. Doing it is doing it.
  • Repeat under real-life circumstances until you don’t need to think about it; condition the mind so behaviors are easy and automatic.
  • Visualize yourself relaxed, genial, warm, friendly, and charming; affirm “I am a completely charming person” with a mental image of enjoying it.

Actions

  • Consciously mirror and match the other person by listening to their speech and watching their body language.
  • Whatever their topic of the day, try to see it from their point of view.
  • Practice and repeat; you need lots of experience.
  • Incorporate these attitudes into listening and speaking habits.
