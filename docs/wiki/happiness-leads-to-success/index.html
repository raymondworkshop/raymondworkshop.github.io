happiness-leads-to-success

[AI Synthesis]: The highlight reverses the usual order. Happiness, in the broad sense of emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment, leads to success. Profit remains the shareholder job, held together with care for employees, clients, and the environment.

Profit alongside care

  • Companies should maximize profits for shareholders while they also care for employees, clients, and the environment.

Where happiness comes from

  • Happiness comes from positive emotions, plus engagement and flow, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment.
  • Maintain positive relationships with the people around you.
  • Engage in tasks that are challenging and that match your capabilities.
  • Find meaning and accomplishment in the work.

Actions

  • When you state what success is, include engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment along with the result.
