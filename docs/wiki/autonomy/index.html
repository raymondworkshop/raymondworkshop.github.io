autonomy

[AI Synthesis]: Autonomy is living with restored awareness, spontaneity, and intimacy—present-moment candor free from compulsive games—and it requires loosening tribal, parental, and cultural scripts even at social cost.

The three capacities

  • Autonomy shows up as the expression or recovery of three abilities: awareness, spontaneity, and intimacy.
  • Autonomous state means building those three capacities; only then can someone pursue genuine liberation.

Awareness

  • Awareness requires being here and now.
  • The aware person is alive because he knows how he feels, where he is, and when it is; he knows that after he dies the trees will still be there but he will not be there to look at them again, so he wants to see them now with as much poignancy as possible.
  • He is living in the present moment with the environment that is here—the sky and the trees as well as the feeling of motion.

Spontaneity

  • Freedom to choose and to express Parent, Adult, and Child feelings in varied ways.
  • Liberation from the compulsion to play games and from having only the feelings one was taught to have.
  • Liberation of one's own native feelings, no longer steered by others' prescriptions.

Intimacy

  • An aware person's natural candor, unfiltered by games—intuitive perception freed, innocence of an uncontaminated Child ego state, living in the present.
  • Intuitive perception evokes emotion; candor can also drive positive feeling.
  • They encourage the other person to speak freely by inviting direct eye contact.
  • Intimacy is a natural Child expression; without game interference the outcome is mostly positive—most infants seem loving.
  • Intimacy degrades when adapted to Parent ego-state demands.

Path to autonomy

  • Exclude the grip of tribe or family tradition.
  • Discard influences from parents, society, and culture—fully reckon with modern social demands.
  • Gain personal and social control.
  • Accept partial or total sacrifice of benefits from one's current social circle.

Actions

  • Once a day, pause to name feeling, place, and time before acting—train Adult awareness of the present environment.
  • When a exchange feels scripted, choose one spontaneous, game-free response (a direct feeling or question) instead of the usual move.
