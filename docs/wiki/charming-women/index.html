charming-women

[AI Synthesis]: Charm a woman by being captivated by her as a person: affection, attention, respect; questions about her life; quiet attentive listening; courtesy; find something to be impressed by.

Emotional connection

  • Caring and concern, plus confidence and competence in the world.
  • Be totally captivated by her as a person and by every word she says.
  • Ask about herself, her day, life, concerns, hopes, background, goals; listen quietly and attentively.
  • Genuine concern for her feelings and experience creates a powerful bond.
  • Be drawn by her ideas, opinions, character, and personality.

Actions

  • Offer affection, attention, and respect.
  • Talk and listen as if she is the most fascinating person you ever met.
  • Extreme courtesy and respect; ask questions and listen closely.
  • Acceptance, appreciation, and approval — ask about work and personal life; find something to be impressed or amazed by.
