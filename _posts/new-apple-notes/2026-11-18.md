Title: #self
---

Thinking mind 
    - decisions rest on facts and evidence.
       It examines everything calmly, analytically, and objectively

Feeling Mind
    - Decisions follow moods and personal experience.
       It reacts fast, and impulses steer the choice.

Wise Mind
     - Thoughtful insight meets calm awareness.
        Logic and emotion are balanced, so choices feel right and work I practice
