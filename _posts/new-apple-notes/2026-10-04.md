安全感機制 在面對熟人時， 啟動了防禦機制

害怕嫉妒與嘲笑， 勝過 渴望欣賞與鼓勵
    -  篩選 能量圈 - 真正的親密關係和友情， 應該是 “可以安心做自己，而不用擔心被射中的地方

    -   儀式感 是給自己和愛人的， 不是給周圍那些帶著放大鏡看戲的人看到

    - 別人如果嘲笑或嫉妒，那是他們內心的匱乏和對自己生活的失控，那不是你的責任。你的人生、你的幸福，不需要透過獲得所有熟人的「審核通過」來證明它的價值。

比你強的人，往往更欣賞真誠，願意學習的人，而不是一個永遠逞強，拒人之外的孤島
    - “這方面我不懂，可以教教我嗎？”

面對比你弱的人
    - 完美主義的心裡防禦， 可能覺得幫人要幫到底，或者幫得完美
    -  幫助別人，從來不需要“拯救”對方；
        有時候，一個傾聽，一個鼓勵，就已經是很好的支持

從“不帶評判的關係”開始
    - 尋找那些 氣場溫和，懂得尊重邊界，不亂評價別人的人
    -  接受關係不完美的，緊密關係不是完美的互相拯救，而是兩個不完美的人，在互相靠近時感到安心

When you play with a finite mindset in an infinite game,
When you play to win in a game that has no finish line,
Decline of trust, decline of cooperation, decline of innovation

In the Infinite Game, the primary objective is to keep playing.
We can't choose the game. We can't choose the rules. We can only choose how we play.

Human skills  
    - listening, giving and receiving feedback

    -  practice being the last to speak
       + coming into a meeting, stating a problem, not giving an opinion, not playing poker, not letting anybody know what you think as they speak, asking questions only out of curiosity, literally be the last to speak, Not only do you make people feel like their opinions matter, but you get the benefit of new ideas, you can still make the same decision you wanted to make at the beginning, but you get all that additional benefit. 

A team is a group of people who trust each other

A leader 
    - recognises that they have a responsibility to see those around them rise
    - It’s not about I did all the work
    - what’s fascinating is a team of average performers is outperform a team of high performers
    - are you in service to your team or yourself

    - that’s showing up for the team and service team 

    - yes, it is unfair you did all the work and they got a good grade

    - the hard work of team is the hard work of learning to build deep meaningful relationships

    - you get skills to be a better listener, a better team player in relationships

Friends are two people who agree to grow together
    - taking oneself on and being supportive of someone else in their growth, sometimes cheering, sometimes giving the tough love
